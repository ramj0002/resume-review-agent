Junior Data Analyst — Fictional Fintech Pte Ltd (Singapore)

About the role
You will analyse product and customer data to help our lending team make better decisions.

Responsibilities
- Write SQL and Python to answer product questions.
- Build and maintain dashboards and metric definitions.
- Help design and read A/B tests.
- Explain findings to non-technical partners.

Requirements
- Degree in Engineering, Computer Science, Statistics or a related field.
- Solid SQL and working Python.
- Good grasp of statistics and hypothesis testing.
- Clear communication.

Nice to have
- Experience with a visualisation tool (Power BI, Tableau).
- Interest in fintech or lending.
